How the City composes

A recording is a fixed file: the same sound every time. The City's own tracks are recipes instead. Your browser performs them live, a sixteenth of a bar at a time. Nothing is downloaded but the recipe, and no two performances are quite the same. Press play and watch it think.

A recipe, not a recording

An mp3 stores the sound itself: millions of numbers, the same every time, megabytes to download. These tracks store only rules: a tempo, four chords, which instrument plays on which sixteenth. Your browser follows them and makes the sound on the spot, with Tone.js.

Sound from scratch

Every instrument starts as a raw wave from an oscillator, or as noise. A filter takes away some of its brightness, an envelope shapes it in time, and echo and reverb give it a room.

Rules and dice

The chords, the tempo and the order of the sections are fixed. Some details are left to chance: is this arpeggio note skipped, which notes make the next melody, will a raindrop fall. So it's always the same piece, never the same performance. Turn Chance down to 0 to hear it play like a recording.
